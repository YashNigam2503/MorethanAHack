# Technical document — <TEAM NAME>

More Than A Hack 2027 · BAR-ATH possession segmentation · submitted file: `<team_name>.csv`
(regenerated by `python possession_model.py --dead-from-missing --viterbi`, see §6).

## 1. Approach

The system labels every tactical-video frame H, A, D or X with a **hand-written, untrained
decoder**. No model is fitted to anything: no labelled data about this game may be used for
training or validation, and the public 5-minute window is too small to select parameters on
without overfitting it. Every parameter is therefore a stated engineering default or a football
prior, written in the code next to its justification.

The pipeline has four stages, each a separate script with validated inputs and outputs:

1. **`stage2_features.py` — tracking validation and geometry.** Checks that the ATD has exactly
   one record per video frame (ids 1..N in order, no duplicates or gaps), that every track slot
   belongs to one of the two metadata teams, and that the tagged kickoffs land on the published
   kickoff frames. It converts normalised coordinates to metres (105.3 × 68.0 m) and caches, per
   frame: ball position, and the distance from the ball to the nearest visible slot of each team.
   It also writes per-frame player-speed summaries (median and 90th percentile of the documented
   per-slot speed in m/s).
2. **`possession_evidence.py` — evidence layer.** Turns geometry into observations (which team
   is strictly nearer, by how much, whether both teams are visible) and Smart Tagging into
   boolean per-frame evidence using an explicit allowlist of the 14 documented codes (§4.2).
   Nothing here decides possession.
3. **`possession_model.py` — decoding.** A per-half **Viterbi decode over H/A/D** with
   transition costs from football priors and emission costs from the evidence (§4.3). Global
   decoding was chosen over frame-by-frame rules because the ranking metric scores whole
   possession spells: a decoder that weighs the entire half produces far fewer spurious
   sub-second switches than one that commits greedily.
4. **Dead-ball rules** feed the same decoder: ball persistently outside the pitch, ball still
   inside a SET PIECES window, and a sustained missing-ball span with calm players (§4.4).

An earlier greedy, causal decoder (confirmation counts per evidence type) is kept in the code as
the default mode; it is also what defines the X frames of the submitted file (§4.5).

## 4. Possession rules

### 4.1 Geometry observations

Per frame, three routes, strongest first:

- **Strong geometry:** both teams visible, nearest team within **2.0 m** of the ball and at least
  **1.0 m** closer than the other team.
- **Weak geometry:** a single strictly nearer team within 2.0 m (weak margin or only one team
  visible) **and** unique stable tag support for the same team. One-sided visibility is never
  treated as an infinite margin.
- **Tag-only:** no player of either team within 2.0 m (a tie inside the radius counts as close),
  and unique stable tag support.

Anything else — no ball, distant ball, ties, conflicting signals — is no evidence. **Loose balls
and challenges** therefore keep the previous team: a contested ball near two players is a tie or
a weak margin and cannot move possession on its own; only sustained one-sided control can.

### 4.2 Smart Tagging

The 14 codes listed in the organisers' `DATA.md` form an explicit allowlist, matched exactly after
case and whitespace normalisation. DATA.md names the codes but does not define them, so the
interpretation below is ours and is **unverified**:

| category | codes | evidence for |
|---|---|---|
| attacking phase | BUILD UP, PROGRESSION, BALL IN FINAL THIRD, CREATING CHANCES, BALL IN THE BOX, PLAYERS IN THE BOX | the tagged team |
| defensive phase | DEFENDING IN DEFENSIVE / MIDDLE / ATTACKING THIRD | the other team |
| transitions | ATTACKING TRANSITION, DEFENSIVE TRANSITION | recorded, not used for decisions |
| set piece | SET PIECES | set-piece context only; the tagged team is not interpreted |
| context only | LONG BALL, GOALS | no possession meaning |

Attacking and defensive support are combined as a union, not counted as independent votes. If
both teams have support on the same frame the tags give no preference. `N/A`, missing or unknown
team values give no team evidence, and unknown codes are reported, never interpreted.

### 4.3 Viterbi decoding

States H, A, D, decoded independently per half. Costs are negative log-probabilities.

| prior | value | football basis |
|---|---|---|
| expected live possession | 12.5 s | midpoint of a 10-15 s expected possession length |
| expected dead-ball spell | 20 s | roughly 35-40 dead minutes per match over well over 100 restarts |
| turnover vs stoppage | 50 / 50 | no reliable figure assumed: the uninformative choice |
| restart team | 50 / 50 | either team can restart |
| half start | live | as kickoff; starting in D costs one live->dead transition |

Each evidence weight is one transition's cost divided by the number of frames the greedy decoder
needs to confirm the same change (strong 15, weak 25, tag-only 50, outside 5, set-piece still 25,
moving ball against D 8, calm missing span 100 frames at 25 fps). A sustained change is therefore
accepted after the same amount of evidence as the greedy decoder needs, while an isolated burst
must pay for leaving *and* returning and needs about twice as much. Ties break deterministically
(H, then A, then D), so repeated runs are byte-identical.

### 4.4 Dead ball and restarts

Evidence for D:

- ball observed outside the pitch by more than 0.5 m (a single outlier is not enough);
- inside a SET PIECES window, ball within 0.5 m of where it was 0.3 s earlier;
- **ball missing for at least 4 s while players are calm**: median visible-player speed at most
  7 km/h (the upper limit of "walking" in standard speed zones) and 90th percentile below
  14.4 km/h (the start of "running"). Missing ball alone never counts: live occlusions and high
  balls are short, and players keep running during them.

Evidence against D: ball inside the pitch and more than 1.0 m from where it was 0.3 s earlier.
Missing tracking never produces D by itself. A SET PIECES interval is never treated as dead as a
whole, and the set-piece Team tag is never used to assign the restart.

### 4.5 X (abstention)

X is written outside the two published halves and, within each half, until the first team is
supported by evidence. Elsewhere the decoder always commits to H, A or D.

## 5. Limitations

- **Unverified tag semantics.** The meaning of each Smart Tagging code, especially which team the
  DEFENDING codes refer to, is inferred from the names. The tags are automatic and noisy.
- **Untuned parameters.** Every threshold and prior is a stated default. None was validated, and
  the dead-ball share of the output has not been checked against the true share.
- **Dead-ball errors.** Likely false D: slow live phases with a long occlusion, a ball standing
  still in live play inside a SET PIECES window, a ball briefly tracked just off the pitch.
  Likely missed D: short stoppages, and stoppages where players keep moving.
- **Control is a proxy.** Nearest-player distance is not ball control; aerial duels, shielding and
  deflections are only handled by persistence.
- **No video.** Nothing is checked against the image, so tracking failures propagate.
- **With more time:** verify the tag semantics against video by eye (allowed for understanding,
  not labelling), use homography to reject off-camera tracking, and model player configurations
  (team shape, distance to the restart spot) instead of speed percentiles.

## 6. Reproduction

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt               # Python 3.11, numpy 2.4.6, pandas 3.1.1
python stage2_features.py
python possession_evidence.py
python possession_model.py --dead-from-missing --viterbi
python evaluation/validate_submission.py local_cache/submission_v4_viterbi.csv
```

The data bundle goes in `./data` or `MTAH_DATA_DIR`. The output is deterministic; the run's full
configuration is saved as `local_cache/submission_v4_viterbi_config.json`. Synthetic tests:
`python -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_*.py"`.
